Compose three widescreen frames from the same dawn at a small bookshop. Begin outside with a dark green front and a single unlettered window. Next frame a hand turning the shop key from the inside. Finish with the same owner setting three books on a sunlit wooden display. Repeat the green paint and brass hardware, preserve the window geometry, and let the sunlight become gradually warmer.

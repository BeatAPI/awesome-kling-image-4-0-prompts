## Prompt

```text
Create a photographic mockup of one readable warm-white exhibition label on a charcoal museum wall. Use a heading “Clay and Current”, then three exact lines: “Studio North”, “Glazed stoneware · 2026”, “On loan from the maker”. The left edge of each line aligns to the same grid. Make the sign nearly front-facing; a soft raking shadow may appear on the wall but never across the letters.
```
